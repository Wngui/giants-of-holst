# Props

Modelled from code in forge.py; textures painted with FLUX (textures.py).

Sewer townsfolk (refugees, Old Pell): animated figures loaded from the Steam workshop packs "Male Human Commoners"
(id 2960527540) and "Female Human Commoners" (id 2960282601), collected by SnickerToodles; original author unknown.
Not redistributed here, only referenced by URL. For private play.

Brazier fire and light: ToMo's Flames - Fire Effects (Steam Workshop 1114184498), loaded from its
author's Dropbox link. Not redistributed here.

Particle effects (spells, explosions, blood, smoke, fire, fog, rain, storm, candle flames): Steam Workshop packs
"Effects: Lighting, Magic, Smoke, Fire and more" (2554037089) and "Lighting and Effects" (1469725554).
Sounds: "Sound Effects" (Workshop 2113057305) and "Soundscape Assets" (Workshop 3323409166; Left 4 Dead 2 sounds,
Valve). All loaded by URL from Steam, not redistributed here. For private play.
